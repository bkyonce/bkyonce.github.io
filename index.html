About

I'm a first-year computer science student at the University of Michigan, focused on privacy, security, and the careful use of AI. I like work where good engineering and clear communication both matter.

I work as a Tech Help Consultant with U-M Information and Technology Services and attend meetings of the Security Privacy Interaction Lab. I'm also a member of WolvSec, where I've contributed ideas for an upcoming capture-the-flag competition.

Right now I'm building a foundation in C++ and discrete mathematics, and developing practical AI workflows for small businesses in downtown Ann Arbor. Outside of school, I play jazz piano.

AI for downtown businesses

I help small downtown Ann Arbor teams save time on repeat work, such as answering the same questions, scheduling, and drafting replies, with a person approving anything sensitive. I'm running short pilots now.

Leasing offices and brokerages

Leasing season brings the same questions all day. I set up an assistant that drafts replies and keeps tours moving.

  • Drafted answers on availability, pricing, pets, parking, and tour times
  • Tour booking and lead follow-up
  • Maintenance request triage

AI never screens, scores, or denies applicants. A person approves anything about eligibility, and replies stay consistent for every inquirer.

Grounded in frontline support work: clear written answers, documented steps, and knowing when to escalate.

Restaurants and bars

Event weekends swing volume. I turn your sales history and the local event calendar into a plan your team can use.

  • A one-page staffing and prep forecast
  • Review-reply drafts
  • An FAQ assistant for hours, reservations, and private events

A person verifies every allergen detail before it reaches a guest.

Informed by taproom shifts in Ann Arbor, where I saw how event-driven demand strains a small team.

How a pilot works

  1. We talk for 20 minutes and pick one repeat task.
  2. I run a 30-day pilot on one location. Scope and data handling are agreed in writing.
  3. We review response times and hours saved, then decide whether to continue.

Customer and applicant data stays out of consumer chat tools, and my samples use synthetic data. I don't give legal advice; anything with legal requirements goes to a qualified professional.
